Why i like him

I grew up with Thomas it was passed down by my sibilings it went from my sister to my two other brothers and then down with me. Throughout the years little by little i started collecting my own stuff which started with my brothers old collection. I would mainly collect trackmaster and tomy items, i would mainly buy them at flee markets and yard sales and thrift stores but later through the years it was getting hard to find trackmaster items. Thomas was getting less popular year by year, but then i was introduced to FaceBook market place where i struck gold. I found a listing of what i call one of my holy grails "The tomy Thomas ultimate set" but not the basic one, i got the one with mighty mac and the sodor suspention bridge i didnt even know they made a tomy mighty mac until that day.
