    " The Heber Valley Railroad, affectionately known by locals as the "Heber Creeper," runs on a 16-mile stretch of original 1899 Denver & Rio Grande Western track between Heber City and Vivian Park in Provo Canyon. Pulled by vintage diesel-electric locomotives—with the historic 1907 Baldwin steam engine No. 618 also part of its collection [1]—the train winds along the Provo River, crosses Deer Creek Dam, and passes right beneath the Wasatch Mountains. Passengers ride in restored vintage coaches complete with heated cars for winter trips, restrooms, and a concession car, choosing from classic three-hour canyon runs like the Provo Canyon Limited to adventure packages that pair the train with river rafting, horseback riding, or zip-lining. Saved from bankruptcy in the early 1990s by local residents and the state of Utah, the railroad now welcomes over 110,000 riders a year [2, 4] for everything from summer sunset BBQs and fiddle fests to the Halloween Pumpkin Festival and the holiday North Pole Express.".
